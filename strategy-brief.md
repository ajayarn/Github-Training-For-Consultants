## Executive Summary

This brief outlines a market entry strategy for the APAC region. The analysis covers market sizing, competitive landscape, and go-to-market recommendations for FY2027.

Key findings:
- Total addressable market estimated at **$4.2B** by 2027
- Three primary competitors hold **68%** of current market share
- Recommended entry point: **South Korea and Japan** in Q1, with SEA expansion in Q3

---

| Singapore | $340M | 15% | Medium |
| Thailand | $280M | 18% | Medium |
| Indonesia | $1,360M | 22% | High (Phase 2) |

---

## Competitive Landscape

### Player A — Market Leader
- 38% market share
- Strong in enterprise segment
- Weakness: limited SME product offering

### Player B — Challenger
- 18% market share
- Aggressive pricing strategy
- Weakness: customer service quality

### Player C — Niche Player
- 12% market share
- Focused on financial services vertical
- Weakness: narrow product scope

---

## Recommendations

1. **Enter South Korea first** — lowest regulatory barriers, strong digital infrastructure
2. **Partner with local distributors** in Japan to navigate relationship-driven market dynamics
3. **Price at 15–20% discount** to market leader to capture share in Year 1
4. **Invest in customer success** as primary differentiator vs. Player B
